Biotechnology Fundamentals

Work through the questions carefully. After submission, review the explanations to strengthen your understanding.

Question 1

What best describes biotechnology?

Correct Answer: A
Biotechnology applies biological organisms, cells, enzymes, genes, or other biological systems to create or improve products, processes, and technologies. It includes fields such as medicine, agriculture, food production, and environmental science.
Question 2

Which molecule serves as the primary hereditary material in most organisms?

Correct Answer: B
DNA stores hereditary information in most organisms. Its nucleotide sequence contains the information needed for biological development, function, and reproduction.
Question 3

Which technique allows scientists to amplify a specific DNA region rapidly?

Correct Answer: A
Polymerase Chain Reaction (PCR) selectively amplifies a target DNA sequence. It is widely used in diagnostics, research, cloning, genetic analysis, and molecular biology.
Question 4

In recombinant DNA technology, what is the main purpose of a vector?

Correct Answer: A
A vector is a DNA molecule used to carry a desired DNA fragment into a host cell. Plasmids are common vectors in molecular biotechnology because they can replicate inside suitable bacterial cells.
Question 5

Which enzyme is commonly used to cut DNA at specific recognition sequences?

Correct Answer: B
Restriction enzymes recognize specific DNA sequences and cut the DNA at or near those sites. They are important tools in recombinant DNA technology.
Question 6

What is the primary function of DNA ligase in genetic engineering?

Correct Answer: A
DNA ligase joins DNA fragments by forming phosphodiester bonds between adjacent nucleotides. This makes it essential for constructing recombinant DNA molecules.
Question 7

Which microorganism is widely used as a host for recombinant protein production?

Correct Answer: A
E. coli is a widely used biotechnology host because it grows rapidly, is relatively easy to culture, and can express many recombinant proteins when appropriate expression systems are used.
Question 8

What is a genetically modified organism (GMO)?

Correct Answer: A
A GMO is an organism whose genetic material has been intentionally modified using molecular biotechnology. The modification may involve adding, removing, or altering genetic sequences.
Question 9

Which biotechnology branch primarily focuses on using biological systems for medical applications?

Correct Answer: A
Red biotechnology refers broadly to biotechnology applied to healthcare and medicine, including therapeutics, vaccines, diagnostics, and other biomedical applications.
Question 10

Which biotechnology branch is most closely associated with industrial production using microorganisms or enzymes?

Correct Answer: A
White biotechnology, also called industrial biotechnology, uses organisms, cells, or enzymes to manufacture products and improve industrial processes.
Question 11

Why are sterile conditions important during microbial biotechnology experiments?

Correct Answer: A
Contamination can introduce unwanted organisms that compete with the intended culture or alter experimental results. Maintaining appropriate aseptic conditions helps preserve the reliability and reproducibility of the experiment.
Question 12

In a bioreactor, why are parameters such as temperature, pH, and dissolved oxygen monitored?

Correct Answer: A
Biological systems are sensitive to environmental conditions. Controlling factors such as temperature, pH, and oxygen helps maintain suitable growth and product formation conditions in a bioprocess.
Question 13

A researcher wants to determine whether a DNA sample contains a specific target sequence. Which approach would be especially useful?

Correct Answer: A
PCR can selectively amplify a target region when primers are designed to bind sequences flanking that region. Detection of the resulting amplification can indicate whether the target sequence was present.
Question 14

What is the main advantage of using enzymes as biological catalysts in biotechnology?

Correct Answer: A
Enzymes are highly useful because many have strong substrate specificity and can accelerate reactions under conditions that are often compatible with biological systems.
Question 15

Which statement best explains the role of fermentation in biotechnology?

Correct Answer: A
Industrial fermentation uses microorganisms or cultured cells to produce useful compounds such as enzymes, organic acids, pharmaceuticals, and other products under controlled conditions.
Question 16

Why are controls important in a biotechnology experiment?

Correct Answer: A
Controls provide a reference condition. Comparing experimental and control groups helps researchers determine whether an observed change is associated with the variable being investigated.
Question 17

Which factor is most important when selecting a host organism for recombinant protein production?

Correct Answer: A
Host selection depends on the biological requirements of the target protein. Expression level, folding, stability, and post-translational processing can all influence which host is appropriate.
Question 18

Which technique separates DNA fragments primarily according to their size?

Correct Answer: A
DNA fragments move through a gel when an electric field is applied. Smaller fragments generally migrate more readily through the gel matrix, allowing fragments to be separated according to size.
Question 19

A biotechnology company wants to produce a therapeutic protein at large scale. Which factor becomes especially important?

Correct Answer: A
Industrial biotechnology requires processes that can be scaled while maintaining product quality, reproducibility, safety, and appropriate process control.
Question 20

Why is biosafety important in biotechnology laboratories?

Correct Answer: A
Biosafety involves practices and controls designed to reduce biological risks and protect laboratory personnel, the environment, and others who could potentially be exposed.
Question 21

In an interview, you are asked why biotechnology is considered interdisciplinary. Which answer is strongest?

Correct Answer: A
Modern biotechnology integrates multiple disciplines. Molecular biology, chemistry, engineering, bioinformatics, statistics, medicine, and other fields frequently work together to solve biological problems.
Question 22

A researcher obtains unexpected results in an experiment. What should be the best first response?

Correct Answer: A
Unexpected results should be investigated systematically. Researchers should examine controls, methods, measurements, sample quality, and experimental conditions before deciding what the result means.
Question 23

Which statement best distinguishes genetic engineering from selective breeding?

Correct Answer: A
Genetic engineering uses molecular methods to intentionally alter genetic material. Selective breeding changes populations by choosing organisms with desirable inherited traits for reproduction over generations.
Question 24

Why is reproducibility important in biotechnology research?

Correct Answer: A
Reproducibility helps establish confidence in scientific findings. Biological experiments can contain variation, so reproducibility does not necessarily mean every measurement is exactly identical.
Question 25

During a biotechnology interview, which response demonstrates the strongest scientific thinking?

Correct Answer: A
Scientific thinking requires evidence-based reasoning. Strong biotechnology professionals evaluate experimental design, controls, limitations, and alternative explanations before reaching conclusions.
